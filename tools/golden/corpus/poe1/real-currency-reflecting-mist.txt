Item Class: Stackable Currency
Rarity: Currency
Reflecting Mist
--------
Stack Size: 4/10
--------
Unpredictably reflects a rare amulet or ring, breaking it
into two mirrored halves with reflected modifiers
--------
Right click this item then left click a rare amulet or ring to reflect it. Cannot be used on Veiled or Influenced items.
Shift click to unstack.
